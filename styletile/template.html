Logos & marks

The duck lockup is the face of the site and sits in the header on every page. The corporate logo appears only in a few chosen places. All marks are now clean vector files, so they stay sharp at any size.

H

Photography & voice

Real photos for every product. Higgsfield only for the harbor scenes around them. The writing stays short and plainspoken.

Product photos (real)
  • Flat lay or ghost mannequin on a Canvas-colored backdrop, soft daylight.
  • Same 4:5 crop for every product, so the shop grid lines up.
  • One front, one back or on-model, and 1–3 close-ups (tag, stitching, rope handle).
  • A photo for every color of every product.
Harbor scenes (Higgsfield)
  • Working New England harbor: cedar shingles, rope-wrapped pilings, buoys, fishing boats.
  • Overcast or golden hour, muted and warm, a little film grain.
  • Never shows Urban Duck products or lettering. AI garbles text, as the catalog comps show.
